# Privacy Policy – PlexiEnhancer

PlexiEnhancer does not collect, store or send any personal data.

- It runs only on perplexity.ai and works entirely inside your browser.
- Your settings (favorite model, toggles, the list of models Perplexity offers you) are saved in the browser's local extension storage and never leave your device.
- The copy buttons read the answer from the page and, after you click Perplexity's own copy button, from the clipboard, only to put a cleaned-up version back on the clipboard.
- No analytics, no tracking, no ads, no servers of its own. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

Questions: https://github.com/Infraviored/PlexiEnhancer/issues
